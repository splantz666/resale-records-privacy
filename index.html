Resale Records Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Resale Records is an inventory and bookkeeping tool. This policy explains how information is handled when you connect an eBay account.

Information used

When you connect eBay, Resale Records receives the eBay account identifier and username made available by eBay, along with the OAuth credentials needed to maintain your connection. Resale Records also handles information you enter or choose to submit, such as inventory records, sales and expense records, listing details, and item photos.

How information is used

This information is used to maintain your eBay connection, keep your resale records, and create or manage eBay listings when you request it.

Sharing

Listing information and photos you choose to submit are sent to eBay so it can create or manage your listings. The service’s hosting and infrastructure providers may process information as needed to operate Resale Records. Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored with safeguards, including encryption for stored refresh credentials. Access is limited to operating the service. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while needed to provide Resale Records. You can revoke Resale Records’ access from your eBay account’s third-party app access settings. Revoking access stops future eBay authorization but may not delete information already stored by Resale Records. To request deletion of stored information, contact the Resale Records developer.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated if the service’s data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page.
